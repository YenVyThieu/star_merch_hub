About Reaching the Little Sun

Reaching the Little Sun is a star merchandise hub created for fans who want to keep a small piece of the artists they admire close to them.

Our store brings together albums, photocards, lightsticks, apparel and accessories from different artists in one bright and welcoming place.

The name represents the feeling of reaching toward someone who shines brightly in your life, even if they may seem far away.

Keep reaching for your little sun. ☀️
